Three sources, one view

Official IDFM route shapes and boarding-point coordinates are joined to the real-time coverage reference by route and stop identifiers. Click a point to request its reported next departures.

Prediction ≠ observation

Source-record age is shown separately from request time. Records older than two minutes are flagged for inspection; this is a display rule, not a measured accuracy threshold. The provider may also return scheduled information.

Requests are on demand and cached for two minutes. There is no background polling.

Coverage has edges

Boarding points are platforms or stop positions, not unique stations. Routes include their termini outside Paris. Closure simulations and operational reliability scores are not part of this first version.
