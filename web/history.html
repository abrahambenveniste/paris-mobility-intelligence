Notice history

Explore what was published and how saved notice content changed.

Reads your local archive only. No PRIM request and no background collection.

Collection times are not incident start/end times. A notice disappearing does not prove resolution. Station-only notices may be excluded by our line-level filter.

How to collect more history

Keep the explorer running and open Start Notice History.cmd. It checks line 4 every five minutes until you close it or press Ctrl+C. Then refresh this page.

Versions are shown newest-first by first collection time, up to 200 matching versions. Repeated sightings of identical content share a version. Returning to an earlier version does not create a new content version. Counts include cached sightings and are not incident counts.

Reported dates without offsets remain in Europe/Paris time; collection timestamps are labelled UTC. No actual incident duration is calculated.
