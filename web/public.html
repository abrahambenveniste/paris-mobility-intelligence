About the data

Routes and stations are from Île-de-France Mobilités and stop and line references. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors. Reference datasets are provided under the ODbL.

Journey and line notices come from PRIM and are subject to source terms. Station groups do not verify transfers; point coordinates are not verified station entrances.
